How to Export a WhatsApp Chat

Tip: Export with media and upload the .zip file to see images, stickers, and videos inline.
  1. Open WhatsApp and go to the chat you want to export.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top right.
  3. Tap More → Export chat.
  4. Choose "Include media" (up to 10,000 messages + media) or "Without media" (up to 40,000 messages, text only).
  5. Share the exported .zip file to yourself (e.g., via Google Drive, email, or Files app).
  6. Come back here and upload the .zip file — images, stickers, and videos will appear inline!
If you exported without media, upload the _chat.txt file directly. Media will show as "Media omitted" placeholders.
WhatsApp Web / Desktop allows exporting individual chats as well.
  1. Open WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) or the WhatsApp Desktop app.
  2. Open the chat you want to export.
  3. Click the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top right of the chat.
  4. Click Export chat.
  5. Choose "Attach media" or "Without media".
  6. Save the exported .zip or .txt file to your computer.
  7. Upload it here to view your chat!
Tip: iPhone exports use a slightly different date format — the viewer handles both formats automatically.
  1. Open WhatsApp and go to the chat you want to export.
  2. Tap the contact name or group name at the top.
  3. Scroll down and tap Export Chat.
  4. Choose "Include Media" or "Without Media".
  5. Share the exported file to yourself via AirDrop, Files, email, etc.
  6. Upload it here to view your chat!
iPhone exports are usually a .zip file containing a .txt file and any attached media.
